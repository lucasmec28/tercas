# Own Ue125 strong local MSE component

This document records the historical v1 partial release. New calculations now
use the separately reviewed v2 approximate distortional component described in
[UE125_NBR_DISTORTIONAL_20261106.md](UE125_NBR_DISTORTIONAL_20261106.md).
Saved v1 results remain partial and are never upgraded merely by reading them.

This is a new calculation for the public nominal Ue125×50×17×2 contour, ri=2 mm,
not an import of Pierin criticals. No private project data, loads or identifiers
are part of this certificate or its synthetic tests.

## Qualified calculation

`ue_catalog_nbr_bending.qualify(data)` returns a partial certificate only for the
explicitly adopted contour and E=200000, G=77000, fy=300, fu=400 MPa. The nominal,
node, property and material hashes bind the result. Other contours/materials
remain outside this first qualification; invalid edited markers fail closed.

NBR14762:2010 §9.8.2.1(b), Table12(b), permits the nominal-dimension expression
for strong-axis local buckling. Here bf/bw=.4 and D/bw=.136 are inside its domain.
The polynomial gives kl=25.638464. This is the selected Table12 route, not an
attempt to reproduce the separately rounded Table13 values.

Ix comes from the same rounded midsurface contour used for the elastic demand.
W=Wc=Ix/(h/2)=18900.603038546056 mm³ uses the exterior fibre, not the more
favourable midsurface ordinate. The explicit formula parameter ν=.3 does not
replace G=77000 MPa or claim that E/(2G)-1 is exactly .3.

Ml=22424085.04996672 Nmm; λp=.5028530537042412; Wef=W. At fy300 and γ1.1,
MRd,local=5154709.919603469 Nmm. Symmetry makes this scalar strong local result
the same in both bending senses. It does not determine the full bending capacity.

The Table12 calculation is a normative approximation with nominal dimensions,
not a new modal eigensolution of the faceted corners. It supplies local MSE/Ief
and the local reduction input for a separately qualified FLT component only.

## Distortion and weak bending remain open

Table14 uses nominal dimensions and linear interpolation. For bf/bw=.4 and
bw/t=62.5, its required D/bw is .2175 (D=27.1875 mm), while the section has .136.
There is no exemption. This is not an automatic failure; it means a compatible
Mdist or other permitted, qualified method is still needed. The weak axis and
its two compression sides cannot borrow this strong-axis table calculation.

NBR14762:2010 §9.3 allows elastic stability analysis or an adequately correlated
direct approximation. FEM is not specifically required. AISI/Hancock flange-lip
methods are possible research routes, but their elastic assumptions and
correlation must be checked before use under the NBR resistance curve. No EN or
AISI resistance is converted into NBR Mdist here. Old catalogue values remain
inactive, even when the user edits their numeric value or free-text provenance.

## Integration and saved evidence

The strong-critical guard consumes only the new Ml and records the certificate
under `ue_catalog_nbr_bending_evidence` in its qualification audit. Mdist remains
null with the precise Table14 reason. Root fields and the historical inputs are
not overwritten. The roof adapters may also save the certificate independently.

`validate_saved(evidence, data)` verifies the pinned marker/material and repeats
only deterministic scalar arithmetic. It does not reopen catalogues, regenerate
a mesh or solve a member. Rehashing altered resistances, adding an exemption,
promoting weak capacities or changing the scope flags is rejected.

No whole-member approval, combined-stress law, weak-axis resistance or complete
bending denominator is granted. Source hash identity is a software provenance
check, not a product certification or independent physical validation.

## Simplified distortional research conclusion

The current primary [ANSI/SDI/AISI S100-2024 and Commentary](https://sdi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/ANSI-SDI-AISI-S100-2024-SDI-AISI-S100-2024-C.pdf)
was checked visually at Commentary §2.3.3.2(c), printed page2-46/PDF page504.
Its short simplified distortional formula is explicitly a preliminary-design
method, transferred to the Commentary in2010, and its bending domain retains
45°≤θ<90°. The orthogonal lip here is not within that literal interval. This is
not an OCR inference from the2007 mirrored text, nor a claim that90° lips cannot
be assessed analytically. The general Appendix2 flange-lip/web formulation
remains a candidate, including its separate provisions for weak bending.

Before importing an elastic result from that route into a NBR resistance curve,
the implementation must establish its geometry conventions and correlation.
The [official2009 S100-07 errata](https://buildusingsteel.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/CFSD-Pub-Errata-to-S100-v1-Amended-6-4-09-High-FINAL.pdf)
corrects the historical hx/xo/yo definitions, so old formulations cannot simply
be copied without reconciliation. The primary [AISI research report RP07-2](https://www.cfsei.org/assets/docs/research_report/RP07-2.pdf),
section11, printed pages11.108–11.111, provides an independent worked benchmark
for the historical general method. That benchmark has not been reproduced by
this change. No approximate Mdist or transferred AISI resistance was released.
