Implemented contracts
- ue_catalog_wall_girt_reference_v1 binds the exact catalogue nominal geometry,
  material and properties to wall_girt. A purlin marker fails after an application
  toggle until explicit re-adoption; old inputs are not edited in place.
- wall_girt_isolated_vlasov_choice_v1 uses the existing torsion_model container,
  manual load_lines records, origin, declared flag and xy_from_CG_mm. The earlier
  purlin declaration remains inactive until the girt choice and each line are
  explicitly adopted. EXTERNAL denotes the already-combined direct q, P its
  own independent line. Area mode keeps CP, winds and their existing factors.
- Coordinates remain in the own contour reference: +x from web toward opening
  (vertically down with mouth down), +y horizontally right. Positive normal
  is horizontally left. Forces are [parallel,-normal]. Coordinates are offsets
  from CG, not offsets from shear centre; the solver retains the distinct SC.
- PP is once at CG, exclusively parallel/vertical. Since CG and SC share y,
  its cross product torque is zero (roundoff aside), not a blanket no-torque
  assumption. A normal force at its independently declared line retains torque.
- Physical span and explicitly adopted ideal forks/free warping govern the
  isolated Vlasov model. No physical support is inferred from Ly/Lz or ties.
  All declared local forces, including parallel forces, remain in demand.
- The roof_top_load choice is preserved but inactive on the girt. No façade
  contact point, top face, sheeting stiffness or sheeting restraint is inferred.

  Legacy CT/no-primary-torque route_domain is cleared in the consumed spec so
  eccentric lines cannot accidentally retain an old CT conclusion.
