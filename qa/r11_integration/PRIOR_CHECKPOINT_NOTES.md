# Scope of earlier integration receipts

`en_representative_results.json` and its log record six synthetic cases before
the v3 EN stress applicability guard. They are retained as historical test
evidence, not as evidence that current normal-stress/interaction checks are
qualified when shear lag or curling is still pending. The current family
application matrix uses a different file, declares its source and inventories
all completed and pending components without converting preparation to approval.

Counts from overlapping focused runs are never added as unique tests.
