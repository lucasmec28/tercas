CONTRACT AND MATHEMATICS

- Only the exact own 134-node rounded contour at catalogue index 26 is admitted.
  Geometry authority includes gross properties, omega and shear primitives,
  not merely a node hash or catalogue dimensions.
- Analysis E/G=200000/77000 MPa remains separate from resistance E=210000 MPa,
  nu=0.3, fyb=fya=300 MPa and gammaM0=1. The previous seven/83 fy250 authorities
  neither authorize this material nor receive changed capacities.
- EN physical My is opposite to app-NBR My. No second sign inversion occurs.
  The four signed effective operators retain c0 and centroid transport.
- Sigma from effective bending plus gross B, and shear from V/Tw and opposite
  Saint-Venant faces, share the same longitudinal and contour coordinates.
  Signed-operator unions and tensor Bernstein controls provide sufficient
  bounds. A control is not a physical witness; an upper bound above one is
  inconclusive, not proof of physical failure.
- EN6.27 uses the separately qualified signed normal floors and both shear
  floors, N exactly zero and Mf=0. No fictitious axial capacity, T-to-V
  conversion or T=0 prerequisite is introduced.
- Shear-lag applicability uses exact rational moment-zero zones, not mesh
  elements or an arbitrary split at P. Curling uses a 300 MPa panel-stress
  bound and minimum distances over all four effective centroids. It does not
  reuse the old gross-centroid fy250 proof or introduce an additional combined
  N/M/B criterion.
- ELS-only inventories cannot qualify an ELU component. High-rotation warnings,
  first-order limitations and global/physical approval flags remain separate.

FINDINGS CLOSED IN THE REVIEWED TREE

1. Re-summarizing stored critical states alone did not authenticate B/T
   coefficients. The new arithmetic replay reconstructs K*u-f without solving,
   verifies element actions and recovered polynomials, rechecks original
   numerical diagnostics, reactions, critical states and rotation witnesses.
   It preserves the original numerical tolerance and physical-load interval
   guard; neither is presented as a normative physical-rotation limit.
2. Canonical comparisons and explicit numeric types reject bool/number aliases
   and rehashed section/control substitutions. The complete pinned section is
   authenticated, including before a warm-cache return.
3. Before-P support-limit states are checked both independently and against
   their prefix in the original critical-state inventory. Resource limits are
   checked before allocating the arithmetic replay matrix.
4. The applied input contract requires zero additional couples, fixed twist
   with free assumed warping at both ends, and the original 0.05 rad warning
   setting marked non-normative. A coherent field for different restraints,
   additional couples or suppressed warnings cannot inherit this opt-in.
5. The bounded read cache accepts only complete successful replays. Its key
   covers canonical full inputs; authorities are checked before hits. RLock
   protects cache lookup/eviction. A refreshed self-hash is not authority.
