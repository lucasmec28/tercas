FORMULA TRANSCRIPTION
F2.1-2, p48/PDF93 and definition p49/PDF94: Y=My=Fy*Sf. Sf refers to the
extreme fiber first reaching yield, in either tension or compression. It is not
necessarily Sfc, which refers specifically to the compression extreme.
F2.2-1/2, p50/PDF95: Mne=Mp*(1-.25*Mp/Mcre) when Mcre>.5*Mp;
otherwise Mne=Mcre. Mp=Fy*Zf.
F3.2-1 to -5, p52/PDF97: barM=min(Mne,My); lambda_l^2=barM/Mcrl;
ks=Mp/My; Mnl=min[ks*barM*(1+.10*alpha_l*lambda_l^2)/(1+.55*beta_s*lambda_l^2),My3].
F4-1 to -3, p54-55/PDF99-100: lambda_d^2=My/Mcrd;
Mnd=min[Mp*(1+.07*alpha_d*lambda_d^2)/(1+.60*beta_s*lambda_d^2),My3].
For both, My3=Mp-(Mp-My)/9; beta_s=max(.4,2*yc/d). Each slenderness must
not exceed5. For this no-hole reference, available resistance factor phi=.90.

GLOBAL WEAK REFERENCE
Appendix2 2.3.1-1,-3,-6,-7, p2-3/2-4, PDF208/209; 2.3.1.2.2-1,
p2-8/PDF213:
Pex=pi^2*E*Ix/(Kx*Lx)^2; T=G*J+pi^2*E*Cw/(Kt*Lt)^2; Pt=T/r0^2.
j=integral(x^3+x*y^2)dA/(2Iy)-x0, with x0=SCx-CGx.
Mcre=Cb*Pex*[Cs*abs(j)+sqrt(j^2+T/Pex)].
Cs=+1 for compression on the shear-center side, -1 for tension there.
The abs(j) bars were verified visually; extracted text loses these bars.
For Cs=-1 compute Mcre=Cb*T/[sqrt(j^2+T/Pex)+abs(j)] to avoid cancellation.
The registry defines x0=CGx-SCx, opposite to the AISI variable; its j already
has the correct combined value. Pex uses Ix STRONG, not Iy weak.
Current reference: Cb=Kx=Kt=1; Lx=Lt=L; ideal fork supports; warping free;
no sheeting or intermediate restraints; uniform pure end moments; N=0.
App2.3 p2-2/2-3, PDF207/208, restricts the analytic route to its geometry/
boundaries; transverse-load expressions assume shear-center application.
Do not equate this reference to a real eccentric-load, combined-stress or
changed-end-bimoment critical factor merely by taking Cb=1.

GEOMETRY, SENSE AND ALPHA
The depth perpendicular to WEAK bending is50mm, not100mm. yc changes from
web distance to lip distance. beta_web=0.709483...0.714364;
beta_lips=1.285636...1.290517. My uses the farther lip exterior fiber in both
senses; that fiber is in tension when the web is compressed.
F3.2 alpha_l and F4 alpha_d have different corner-displacement requirements.
The algebraic floor removes their nonnegative reserve terms. This does not
claim that either actual alpha is zero, nor that local alpha equals D alpha.
The mode's corner translation cannot be inferred solely from the section name.

DERIVED FLOOR, NOT A LITERAL NORMATIVE EQUATION
P=F2.2 evaluated at Mp=My.
L=P/(1+.55*beta_s*P/Mcrl).
D=My/(1+.60*beta_s*My/Mcrd).
Ma_floor=.90*min(P,L,D) when F4 applies. For the independently qualified
pure weak-web component below, Ma_floor=.90*min(P,L) because F4 is not
applicable. That is a limit-state applicability decision, not D=infinity.
F2.2 is monotone in Mp up to its plateau, so P<=min(Mne,My). The function
x/(1+a*x) is increasing for a>0. Removing ks>=1 and positive alpha terms
therefore gives a lower value of F3.2 and F4. All floor branches are <=My,
while My3>=My. This proof holds for beta_s>0, not only beta=1.
The code uses sqrt(My/Mcrl)<=5, a sufficient bound on the REAL local
slenderness; checking sqrt(P/Mcrl)<=5 alone would not establish the domain.
Independent analytic review is in independent_review/REVIEW.md.

H4 INTEGRATION GATES
1. Resolve physical compression side, not an unqualified sign. The saved FSM
   uses sigma_compression=-My*x/Iy: positive My compresses the web. Vlasov's
   sigma_tension=-My*x/Iy means positive My compresses the lips. Helpers differ
   intentionally. Zero weak moment has no compressed side.
2. Reference capacities do not verify combined-demand stability. Real support,
   load-height, moment variation, warping restraints and C1 must be justified.
3. These are principal-axis denominator references, not the resultant-axis
   Mcre of Appendix2.3.1.2.5 nor a generalized-stress buckling solution.
4. B4.1 dimension gates pass; A3 material/ductility and B7 actual delivered
   thickness remain unverified. No cold-work increase, holes or tension-only
   restraint is introduced. No girt/tie-rod design is performed.
5. No revised H4, Wagner shear recovery, G8.2 completion, serviceability,
   connection/local load introduction or whole-member approval is claimed.
